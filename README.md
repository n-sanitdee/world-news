# World News & Views (ข่าวโลก มุมมองเรา)

Activity page for **World News & Views**, a department-level project (โครงการระดับสาขา) of the
Department of English for Professional and International Communication (EPIC),
School of Humanities, University of the Thai Chamber of Commerce — academic year 2569.

**Live page:** https://n-sanitdee.github.io/world-news/

## What the activity is

A weekly, student-led English news circle. Students choose any English-language news item or
article that interests them, present it to the group, and discuss it together. One hour per week,
capped at 20 participants, run either on-site or online depending on what suits people that week.

Alongside language practice, sessions build **critical thinking** and **media and information
literacy** — checking where information came from, separating fact from claim, and looking at an
issue from more than one angle. Students collect their work into a Learning Portfolio.

## Who the page is for

The page serves two audiences in one link:

- **Students** (top half) — what the activity is, what they get out of it, how to join, and FAQ.
- **Lecturers considering joining the committee** (bottom half) — full project brief, objectives,
  KPIs, evaluation plan, and an honest account of the workload involved.
